# Vision

PNMF enables conceptual aircraft—before certification data exists—to be
screened in the established ANP/Doc-29 NPD workflow.

It combines a provenance-controlled EASA ANP training corpus, two supported
tree-ensemble predictors, and an independent component-source physics check.
The target users are aircraft/noise researchers, not certification authorities.
The framework supports comparative design screening; certification prediction
and regulatory compliance claims are out of scope.
